# Delivery Records

This folder records public-safe delivery evidence for the CYBERSEC 2026 AI SaMD talk after the talk package has left rehearsal and entered external-facing use.

Use it for:

- organizer-provided talk outcome reports
- post-event evidence summaries
- repo-safe delivery and acceptance records
- pointers to local-only raw source captures

Do not use it for:

- raw private email archives
- private contact details
- credentials, signed identity documents, or payment records
- unpublished hospital, client, student, or patent-sensitive material

## Maintenance Rule

Keep this folder evidence-led and bounded. Track the outcome facts, scores, source fingerprints, and delivery implications that are useful for future talks. Keep raw email exports and private administrative evidence in `.local/` or the planning/source-evidence layer unless the owner explicitly approves a public-safe export.
